Cancellation policy

Clear cancellation terms.

Before dispatch

Request cancellation using your order ID before dispatch. We will confirm whether cancellation is possible.

Prepaid orders

For an eligible cancellation, the prepaid amount may be refunded after review, using the original payment method where possible.

COD advance

The ₹199 COD advance is part of the order value, not an extra charge. Its refund treatment for an eligible cancellation is reviewed according to order stage, fraud checks and applicable law.

After dispatch and failed delivery

Orders may not be cancellable after dispatch. If delivery fails because of an incorrect address, repeated failed attempts or refusal, the COD advance may be handled as disclosed during checkout and subject to applicable law.

How to request

Contact Coverly support with your order ID, phone number and reason. This policy should be reviewed for your actual business practices before launch.
