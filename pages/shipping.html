Shipping & Delivery

Fast & safe door-step delivery.

We partner with India's leading courier services including BlueDart, Delhivery, and Xpressbees to deliver your custom case safely.

Dispatch & Delivery Timelines

• Order Processing & Print QA: 1–2 working days
• Estimated Delivery Time: 3–5 working days depending on your city and pincode
• Shipping Charges: Free standard delivery across India on all orders

Live Tracking

As soon as your package is handed over to our courier partner, tracking details and AWB numbers are updated in your order. You can track your shipment anytime on our Track Order page using your Order ID and mobile number.

Address Accuracy & Delivery Attempts

Please ensure complete house, building, street, and landmark details along with a 6-digit pincode at checkout. Couriers will make up to 3 delivery attempts. For delivery assistance or change of address before dispatch, contact Coverly Support promptly.
