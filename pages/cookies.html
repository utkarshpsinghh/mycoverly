Cookie Policy

How we store your bag & order preferences.

We keep cookie and storage usage transparent and minimal, focusing solely on smooth checkout and order continuity.

Necessary local storage

Coverly uses browser local storage to maintain your custom shopping bag, selected phone model, and recent order history across page refreshes. Clearing browser storage will reset your local bag and cached order records.

Analytics & Measurement

We use standard Google measurement tags (gtag.js) to understand aggregate store traffic, performance, and visitor trends without storing confidential personal data.

Managing your preferences

You can manage or clear cookies and local storage through your browser settings anytime. Disabling local storage may prevent the cart from retaining items during browsing.
