Clear about your information.

How we collect, use, and protect your personal information when you order custom cases from Coverly.

Information collected

We may collect your name, phone number, email address, delivery address, order details, UTR/reference numbers, device/browser information, and customer care messages. We never ask for UPI PINs, bank passwords, or OTPs.

Use and sharing

Your information is used strictly for processing orders, customer support, shipping, payment verification, security, and legal obligations. Relevant details are shared only with essential delivery and hosting partners to fulfill your orders.

Retention, security and rights

We retain order information only as needed for order fulfillment, warranty, and accounting purposes. We apply standard security safeguards to protect your data. You may request correction or deletion of your contact data by emailing support@mycoverly.store.
