Eligible returns

Unused, undamaged items in original packaging may be requested within 7 days of delivery.

Used, altered, personalised or physically damaged items are not eligible unless required by law.

Refund timing

Approved refunds are issued to the original payment method within 5–7 business days.

How to request a return

  1. Email support@coverly.store with your order ID and reason.
  2. Wait for written approval and return instructions.
  3. Pack the item securely and send it as instructed.
  4. We inspect it and confirm the refund or replacement.
